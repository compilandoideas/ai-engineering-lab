# 0007. Vague or evasive replies and any safety advice always go to a human

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Vague or evasive replies and any safety advice always go to a human.

## Alternatives discarded and why

Treating "We're on it" or "It should ship soon" as harmless status updates. They
promise nothing explicit, which is exactly why the customer reads a promise into them. The judge
prompt names them explicitly.

## Evidence

in the judge study every escape by gpt-4.1-mini was of this kind (evasive, vague
timing, safety advice) and repeated 5 of 5 runs, so it is a policy gap, not noise.
