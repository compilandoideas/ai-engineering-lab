# 0002. Pause on content, not on the tool

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Pause on content, not on the tool.

## Alternatives discarded and why

"every `send_reply` needs a human" (reviewers rubber-stamp) and "no tool needs a
human" (refund promises go out). The same tool sends a status update or a liability admission;
the risk is in the text, the ticket and the permissions.

## Evidence

`S1_verified_fact_is_sent_without_a_human` vs `S2_refund_promise_waits_for_a_human...`.
